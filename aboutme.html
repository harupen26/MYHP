About Me

I am an undergraduate student specializing in Computer Graphics at Waseda University. My research interests include fabrication and human-computer interaction (HCI). I am especially interested in giving passive fabricated objects new functionality through computational techniques such as simulation, optimization, and machine learning.

I also perform in color guard and marching bands. You can learn more about these activities on my Hobby page.
